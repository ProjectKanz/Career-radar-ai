# Career Radar Evaluation Cases

Use these cases before trusting a CV brain or workflow refactor.

## Case 1: BFLP Business Enabler

Expected role DNA:

- primaryDirection: Generalist Leadership Program
- industrySignals: banking, business enablement
- functionSignals: operations, digital transformation, business analysis
- eligibilitySignals: English/TOEFL, GPA, degree, placement willingness if present

Expected evidence emphasis:

- English Proficiency Test Score Record
- Gemini Certified Faculty
- Financial Analyst Course
- Excel certification
- CSA operations/process control evidence
- XL data/reporting evidence
- AI Workflow Automation reframed as digital innovation

Expected CV sections:

- Professional Summary: MT/business leadership positioning
- Certifications: English first, then digital/finance/reporting credentials
- Achievements: Capital Market Competition if relevant
- Skills: operations, reporting, Excel, business analysis, communication

Avoid:

- Capital Market Competition in Certifications
- over-technical AI wording
- executive leadership claims
- duplicate English or Gemini entries

## Case 2: Danamon Future Leaders Program

Expected role DNA:

- primaryDirection: Generalist Leadership Program
- industrySignals: banking, financial services
- functionSignals: leadership development, branch/business/operations exposure

Expected evidence emphasis:

- GPA and Management degree

- operations discipline and reporting evidence

## Case 3: Data / BI Role

Expected role DNA:

- primaryDirection: Data / BI
- functionSignals: reporting, dashboarding, analysis, monitoring
- hardSkillSignals: Excel, Google Sheets, dashboarding, SQL only if verified

Expected evidence emphasis:

- XL data and digital channel analysis
- Excel and Google Sheets evidence
- dashboard/reporting evidence
- AI Workflow Automation only when framed as workflow/reporting enablement

Expected CV sections:

- Summary: data/reporting analyst positioning
- Work Experience: XL bullets first in relevance
- Skills: evidence-backed analytics/reporting tools

Avoid:

- claiming SQL/Power BI if not evidenced
- generic "data scientist" wording

## Case 4: Operations / Project Admin Role

Expected role DNA:

- primaryDirection: Business / Operations or Procurement / Supply Chain
- functionSignals: administration, documentation, coordination, tracking, reporting

Expected evidence emphasis:

- PT Cahaya Sriwijaya Abadi documentation/material control
- process coordination
- reporting visibility
- stakeholder communication
- Excel if used for tracking/reporting

Expected CV sections:

- Summary: operations/project administration positioning
- Work Experience: CSA bullets emphasized
- Skills: documentation, coordination, reporting, Excel

Avoid:

- excessive AI/digital emphasis
- leadership overclaiming
- unrelated finance achievements unless role asks for finance exposure
